About AEDETV

More Than Entertainment.
A Community of Faith.

Anointed Emmanuel Drums Entertainment Television (AEDETV) is a vibrant space centered on gospel music, live worship performances, comedy, movies, and spiritual enrichment.

We serve as a digital sanctuary where faith meets creativity, providing a front-row seat to powerful live performances, soulful gospel songs, and inspiring content.

Our mission is to spread the Word of God, encourage spiritual growth, entertain, and build a supportive community through music and meaningful content.

AEDETV isn't just a channel; it's a growing community of faith and music.
